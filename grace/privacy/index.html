This policy describes how Grace, an app by Redwood Apps, handles your data. Grace does not collect personal data and does not share it with the developer or any third parties.

Data storage

All your expenses, categories and settings are stored only on your phone. The developer has no access to them.

Data collection

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It does not connect to the internet and does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, location or any other data on your phone.

Backups

If backups are turned on on your phone, Grace data is included in the backup together with data from other apps. The backup is made by the operating system and stored in the cloud of your account. The developer has no access to it. You can turn off backups in your phone settings.

Data export

An export file is created only when you ask for it and is saved wherever you choose. The file is not encrypted, so keep it in a safe place and do not share it with others.

Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, they will receive your email address and the content of your message. This information is used only to reply to you and is not shared with third parties. The email comes pre-filled with the app and system versions, which you can delete before sending if you wish. The app does not detect your phone model. Your correspondence will be deleted at your request.

Deleting data

You can delete all data in the app settings with “Delete data”, or by deleting the app itself. Deleted data can only be restored from your phone backup or from an export file, if one was made.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new update date.
